## Caption

178 weekly international flights. That is Maiquetía today, according to the airline association ALAV.

Still below the 260 recorded before the June earthquakes, but new links keep arriving: Qatar Airways via Bogotá since 4 November, Avior from Barcelona to Miami from 20 November, Copa to Margarita from tomorrow, 26 November, and American to Barcelona from 15 January. And 44% of international departures now leave from outside Caracas, led by Valencia.

For hospitality, seats come before guests.

Which route would you book first?
