## Caption

Most people know Margarita for its beaches. The island has more to show.

In Pampatar, the San Carlos Borromeo fort was completed in 1684, after pirates burned the town, and now works as a museum. To the west, La Restinga National Park, protected since 1974, hides a lagoon with 18 km of mangrove channels where scarlet ibis and flamingos feed. On the south coast, El Yaque gets 15 to 30 knots of trade wind most of the year, over shallow water: a classic for windsurfers (hello, @hotelcasaviva.elyaque).

From 26 November, Copa adds three weekly flights from Panama to Porlamar.

Fort, lagoon or wind: where would you start?
