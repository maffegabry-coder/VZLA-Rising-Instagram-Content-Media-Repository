## Caption

Caracas has 26,000 to 30,000 registered hotel bed places. Bogotá has about 70,000.

That is the estimate of Fenahoven, Venezuela's hotel federation, which says business travel is outpacing supply. Portuguese hotel groups met in Caracas on 3 November, and more investor delegations are expected by early December.

The catch: Fenahoven says taxes, once about 5% of hotel costs, now take up to 50% of revenue at some properties.

Interest is real. So is the homework: demand, tax, title, utilities, sanctions.

Which check would you run first?
