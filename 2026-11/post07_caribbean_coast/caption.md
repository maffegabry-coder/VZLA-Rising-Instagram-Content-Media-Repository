## Caption

Venezuela's Caribbean coast is not one place. It is a route.

Start west in Falcón, where Morrocoy National Park protects 32,090 hectares of cays, mangroves and reef, a park since 1974. In the centre, Henri Pittier, decreed in 1937, was Venezuela's first national park: 107,800 hectares where cloud forest runs down to Choroní and the sea, with more than 500 bird species recorded. Further east, Mochima counts 38 islands and islets, and Margarita closes the route with more than 70 beaches.

Which stretch would you start with?
