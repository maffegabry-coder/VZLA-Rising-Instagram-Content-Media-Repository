## Caption

A 1960s sign on Avenida Francisco de Miranda is lit again, via @ecosistemag.

Súper Hollywood, a family dry cleaner in Chacao, opened in 1952. Its sign went up in the early 1960s and, after six decades of sun and wear, was close to disappearing. The beer brand Cardenal took it down, matched the original colours found behind the letters, kept the street mural by the late Don Plin, and swapped neon for LED. The public will nominate the next signs, in Caracas first and then other cities.

For investors, the lesson is simple: character is part of a location's value.

Which Caracas landmark should be next?
