## Caption

US$1,561. That is the typical asking price per square metre for an apartment in Caracas this November.

The HabitaOne index, reported by Bloomberg Línea, shows a city split in two: about US$2,945 per m² in Chacao against US$1,075 in Libertador. Entry-level supply is shrinking too: listings under US$100,000 fell 20% between May and October, and rents per m² rose 14% to 23% from June to October.

These are asking prices, not closed sales. Before any purchase, check the structural report, the title and the running costs.

Chacao or Libertador: where would you look first?
