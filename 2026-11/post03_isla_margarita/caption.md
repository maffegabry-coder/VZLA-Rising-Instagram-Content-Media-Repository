# Post 03 - Isla Margarita: An Underrated Caribbean Destination?

Pillar: Investment Perspectives - Publication: Fri 6 Nov 2026, 18:00 Europe/Rome (12:00 Miami)

## Caption

78 km of island, more than 70 beaches, and a new link to Panama from 26 November.

Isla Margarita has long been Venezuela's best-known beach destination, with a free-port history dating back to the 1970s. Copa Airlines plans three weekly flights between Panama City and Porlamar, connecting the island to one of the region's main hubs.

Where could the opportunity be? Our hypotheses: repositioning existing hotels before building new ones, low-density nature stays around Macanao and La Restinga, and itineraries that combine Margarita with Los Roques.

Before any of that, the basics: land title, sanctions compliance, water and power, permits, currency and real demand data.

What would make Margarita investable for you?
