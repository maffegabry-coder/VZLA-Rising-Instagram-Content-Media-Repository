# VZLA Rising - Brand guide

Positioning: discovering Venezuela's landscapes, transformation and emerging opportunities. Premium editorial, facts first.

## Logo
- Source: founder-approved logo. The wordmark files in `brand/logo/` are extracted from it (lettering and flag only, Angel Falls removed). Never redraw or regenerate the lettering.
- Light backgrounds: `espresso-colorflag` (default) or `mono-espresso`.
- Dark backgrounds and photography: `white-colorflag` (default) or `mono-white`.
- Premium monochrome uses: `mono-black`, `mono-sand`.
- On slides: bottom left, 58 px high, never larger.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Ivory | #F5F0E8 | Default slide background |
| Sand Beige | #E4D6C4 | Alternate background, map fills |
| Stone Taupe | #B7A28D | Accent on dark |
| Earth Brown | #685344 | Accent on light, secondary bars |
| Deep Espresso | #302820 | Covers, closing slides, primary bars |

Pillars: Venezuela Uncovered (ivory/sand), Venezuela in Motion (ivory + espresso covers), Investment Perspectives (ivory + espresso closing).

## Typography
- Headlines and figures: Cormorant Garamond Light, lining numerals.
- Labels, data, sources: Inter, uppercase with wide tracking for labels.

## Layout (1080 x 1350)
- Margins 96 px. Top bar: pillar left, slide number right, hairline below.
- Footer: wordmark left, sources right.
- Rhythm: designed cover, then photo and information slides alternating, closing slide with a question.
- Photography slides are full-bleed real photos of the named place (Pexels or Unsplash licence, or founder photos), never AI-generated.
- Charts are drawn to scale from verified values; every figure has a source on the slide or in the first comment.

## Writing
- English first, Spanish second. Hook in the first line, open question at the end, three hashtags, sources and photo credits in the first comment.
- Never the long dash. Separate words with "-".
- Distinguish confirmed facts, announcements and hypotheses. No investment promises.
