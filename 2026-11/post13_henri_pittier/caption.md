# P13 | Venezuela Uncovered | 2026-11-30 18:00 Europe/Rome

## Caption

Venezuela's oldest national park starts in the cloud forest and ends on the Caribbean.

Henri Pittier was protected in 1937. It climbs from sea level to more than 2,300 metres, and more than 500 bird species live here. Every year migrants cross the range at the Portachuelo pass, at 1,136 metres.

Then the road drops to the coast: Choroní with its colonial village and posadas, the calm water of La Ciénaga near Ocumare de la Costa, and the surf beach of Cuyagua.

One park, three very different days. Which one would you choose: village, bay or surf?
