## Caption

US$1,017 million. That is trade between Colombia and Venezuela from January to September 2026.

According to the Cámara Colombo Venezolana, it is up 17.9% on the same months of 2025, and September alone grew 33.6%. The flow runs mostly one way: Colombian exports reached US$938.8 million (+20.4%), while Venezuelan sales to Colombia fell 5% to US$79.1 million.

Context matters: in 2008 the two neighbours traded more than US$7,000 million. Today's level is well under a fifth of that. Topic spotted via @ecosistemag.
