# Los Roques: hospitality inside a national park

Investment Perspectives - Fri 13 Nov 2026, 18:00 Rome - 6 slides

## Caption

In Los Roques, a building can rise one floor and 5.5 metres. That rule explains the whole business.

The archipelago is a national park of 221,120 hectares, most of it under water. Hospitality here means small lodges: 60 registered in the latest study we found, with surveyed properties between 8 and 38 beds.

Access is its own limit, about 40 minutes from Caracas by small plane. And a 2004 decree suspended new concessions, with a last published extension to 2014, so current status comes first.

Should Los Roques stay small by design?
