# Mérida, the Venezuelan Andes

Venezuela Uncovered - Mon 9 Nov 2026, 18:00 Rome - 6 slides

## Caption

Venezuela has a side most people never picture: cold air, glacial lakes and a cable car to 4,765 m.

The Mukumbarí runs 12.5 km from the city of Mérida to Pico Espejo, and it returned to service on August 19 after months of maintenance. Around it, the Sierra Nevada National Park protects 276,446 hectares, with ten glacial lakes and peaks up to 4,978 m.

The drier months, December to March, are the ones to plan around. Check the cable car schedule before you go.
