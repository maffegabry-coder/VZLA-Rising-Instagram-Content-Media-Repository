# Post 01 - Venezuela: More Than You Imagine

Pillar: Venezuela Uncovered - Publication: Mon 2 Nov 2026, 18:00 Europe/Rome (12:00 Miami)

## Caption

Most people picture Venezuela through headlines. The landscape tells a different story.

In a single country, Angel Falls drops 979 m from a tepui in Canaima National Park. Los Roques protects 221,120 hectares of reefs, islands and cays. Pico Bolívar rises to 4,978 m in the Andes. And in between stretch the vast plains of Los Llanos.

Venezuela is one of the world's 17 megadiverse countries, with about 2,800 km of coastline.

This is where VZLA Rising begins: discovering the places, the transformation and the opportunities, facts first.
