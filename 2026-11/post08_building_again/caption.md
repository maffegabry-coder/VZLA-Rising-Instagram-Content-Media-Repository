# P08 | Venezuela in Motion | 2026-11-18 18:00 Europe/Rome

## Caption

+35%. That is how much Venezuela's construction sector could grow in 2027, according to a new forecast by Summa Consulting reported by Bloomberg Línea.

The base is very low: the sector shrank 81.4% between 2014 and 2025, and Summa projects +15% for 2026. The bigger gap is financing. Bank credit is about 3.2% of GDP, against a regional average near 50%. The construction chamber says the sector plans up to 14,000 homes, and that mortgages need lower bank reserve requirements to return.

Forecasts, with conditions.
