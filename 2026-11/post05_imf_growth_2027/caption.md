# IMF projects 7% growth for Venezuela in 2027

Venezuela in Motion - Wed 11 Nov 2026, 18:00 Rome - 6 slides

## Caption

7%. That is the IMF's growth projection for Venezuela in 2027, second in Latin America and the Caribbean after Guyana (16.8%), via @bancaynegocios.

The regional average is 2.5%. But read the whole table. Venezuela lost more than 70% of its GDP between 2014 and 2021, so this growth starts from a very low base. The same outlook projects average inflation of 174.7% in 2027 and a current account deficit of 3.4% of GDP.

These are forecasts, not results.
